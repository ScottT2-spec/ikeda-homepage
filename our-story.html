Our Story

Fragrance, pursued as one calling since 2002.

"The world is fragrant because of me."

Founded in 2002 — over two decades in fragrance, built on one promise: craft every scent with professional care.

Fragrance is the beauty of the intangible, the warmth of a space, and a ritual of everyday life. Since its founding in 2002, IKEDA has pursued one calling: to craft every comforting scent with professional care, bringing beautiful fragrance into every living scene and every space.

Scent — Craft with Expertise, Leave a Lasting Legacy

For over twenty years, IKEDA has specialized in fragrance. Rather than relying on homogeneous mass production, it creates original compositions and develops its own formulas — from carefully selecting ingredients worldwide to researching fragrance structures and maintaining rigorous safety and quality controls. Every product is refined with precision. We do more than produce scents: we create olfactory memories that last and can be passed on.

Principle — Be Diligent and Considerate; Act with Integrity and Respect

Skill gives the craft its strength; principles give it direction. IKEDA follows a diligent, pragmatic and efficient way of working, serving every customer and global partner with care. Honesty, sound business conduct, worldwide reach and scalable production underpin consistent quality, responsive service and reliable delivery — building lasting trust in the brand.

You — Help Others Succeed to Fulfill Our Own Potential

A brand grows with its customers and partners. IKEDA puts user needs first and seeks shared success through high-quality fragrances and one-stop solutions. By enabling partners to grow, we grow together, creating enduring value for all.

With fragrance as our medium, principles as our measure and mutual success as our purpose, IKEDA remains true to its founding spirit. May every space find its own elegant scent, and may the world be fragrant because of us.
